## 1. What this project is for

We are collecting voice recordings and their written transcripts to build speech datasets. These datasets are used to train and test speech technologies such as **speech recognition (speech-to-text)** and **speech synthesis (text-to-speech)**.

## 2. What we collect

- **Voice recordings** of you reading or speaking sentences.
- **Transcripts**: the text of each sentence.
- **Background details**: year of birth, gender, education, occupation, languages spoken, and your general location (country, state, district and the area of your pincode).
- **Contact details**: your name, mobile number and (optionally) email address.
- **Technical details**: audio settings, microphone type and recording environment.

## 3. How we protect your privacy

- **All metadata is kept anonymous and will not be made public.**
- Your **name, mobile number and email address are never included in the dataset**. They are stored separately (encrypted where configured). We use them only to contact you about your contribution, to confirm your consent, or to process a request to withdraw. They will never be published, sold or shared.
- Your recordings are identified only by an **anonymous Speaker ID** (for example `eng_OD_KHO_M_1999_7f3a9c2e`). This code cannot be turned back into your name or phone number.
- Your exact pincode is never placed in the dataset metadata. Only its first three digits (a broad region) are used.
- Please **do not say personal information** (your name, address, phone number, ID numbers) in any recording.
- Please note that a human voice can be recognisable to people who already know you.

## 4. How your recordings will be used

Your recordings, transcripts and anonymous metadata will be used by {ORG_NAME} to develop, train and evaluate speech and language technologies.

## 5. Your rights

- You can **stop at any time** without giving a reason.
- You can **withdraw** your contribution later by emailing **{CONTACT_EMAIL}** with your Speaker ID. Your recordings will then be removed from future versions of the dataset.
- You can ask what information we hold about you.
